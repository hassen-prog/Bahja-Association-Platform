Why Choose Bahja?

Choose Bahja-Association because we are passionately committed to providing unwavering support, hope, and joy to children fighting cancer, helping them and their families navigate through their toughest times with compassion and care.

Learn More

Compassionate Support

Our association offers compassionate guidance and unwavering support to children battling cancer and their families. From the moment of diagnosis, our team is dedicated to providing emotional, financial, and practical assistance, ensuring that families feel empowered and cared for throughout their journey.

Comprehensive Care

Beyond medical treatment, we prioritize the holistic well-being of each child. Our tailored programs and services address not only their physical health but also their emotional and social needs. From specialized therapies to educational resources, we provide comprehensive care that nurtures every aspect of a child's healing and growth.

Empowering Community

By choosing us, families join a supportive community that understands the challenges of childhood cancer. Through shared experiences, events, and peer support, we create a network of strength and resilience where families can find solace, encouragement, and a sense of belonging.

Services

Emotional Counseling

Providing professional psychological support for children and their families to help them cope with the emotional challenges of a cancer diagnosis and treatment.

Financial Assistance

Offering grants or financial aid to families to help cover the costs of medical treatments, travel expenses, and other related expenses.

Educational Support

Providing tutoring services and educational resources to ensure that children can continue their education during treatment, including home or hospital schooling.

Therapeutic Activities

Organizing art therapy, music therapy, and recreational activities to support the emotional and psychological well-being of children.

Family Support Groups

Facilitating support groups and workshops for parents and siblings to share experiences, gain support, and learn coping strategies.

Awareness and Advocacy

Conducting awareness campaigns, fundraising events, and advocacy efforts to educate the public about childhood cancer and promote better policies and funding for research and treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What types of support do you offer to children and families?

    We offer a range of services including emotional counseling, financial assistance, educational support, therapeutic activities, and family support groups. Our goal is to provide comprehensive care that addresses the physical, emotional, and social needs of children and their families.

  • How do you help children continue their education during treatment?

    We provide educational support through tutoring services, home or hospital schooling, and collaboration with schools to ensure that children can keep up with their studies during treatment. Our educational coordinators work closely with families and schools to create personalized education plans.

  • What are the qualifications of your staff?

    Our staff includes experienced professionals in various fields such as oncology, psychology, social work, and education. Each team member is dedicated to providing compassionate and expert care tailored to the needs of children with cancer and their families.

  • What resources are available for newly diagnosed families?

    For newly diagnosed families, we offer a comprehensive orientation program that includes informational packets, one-on-one counseling sessions, and connections to support groups. We aim to provide the necessary guidance and support to help families navigate this challenging time.

  • How can I get involved or volunteer with your association?

    There are many ways to get involved, from volunteering at events to providing administrative support or helping with our awareness campaigns. Visit our "Get Involved" page to learn more about volunteer opportunities and how you can make a difference.
